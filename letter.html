A LETTER 08 / 2026
Dear Ritika, ♥

I don't think I say this enough, but having you as my elder sister is one of those things I know I'll always be grateful for.

We've had our share of arguments, stupid fights, annoying each other for absolutely no reason and pretending we weren't going to talk to each other again.

And yet somehow, you're still one of the first people I know I can count on.

You probably don't realise it, but a lot of the things you do have quietly stayed with me.

So this Raksha Bandhan, I didn't want to just send you another message.

I wanted to make you something.

Something small. Something completely unnecessary. Something that exists only because you're my sister.

Happy Raksha Bandhan, Ritika.

Your irritating
younger brother.
